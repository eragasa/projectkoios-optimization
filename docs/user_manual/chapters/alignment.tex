\chapter{alignment}

Alignment selects mathematical transformations between prepared representations.
Optimization owns transformation selection and numerical evidence. It does not own the
physical or scientific meaning of the representations.

\section{Ownership}
PhysKit may adapt units, operators, lattices, hoppings, and frame identities into
unit-free arrays. KSD may select scientific controls and interpret evidence. These
consumer responsibilities do not enter Optimization.

\section{Available families}
The library provides one-pair complex-unitary right Procrustes alignment and a dense
NumPy batched family. Both act on the right of the source and share one generic numerical
policy owner.

\section{Evidence and interpretation}
Rank, residual, cutoff, and backend metadata are software evidence. Consumers must not
infer scientific validity from full rank or a small residual alone.
